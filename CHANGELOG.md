# Panthers & Eagles changelog

## 2.1 (November 24, 2026)
- Better sorting of Oak Grove listings that use general calendar names: concerts and color guard now count as band and choir, the FFA banquet as academic, and board meetings and early-release days as school events.

## 2.0 (November 24, 2026)
- Refreshes itself every night: reads Oak Grove's activities calendar about 45 days ahead and Central Methodist's cheer and STUNT calendar feeds, then updates the page.
- Central Methodist's first 2026-27 dates now show: NCA National Championships in Daytona Beach, April 8 (preliminaries) and April 9, 2027 (finals).
- The top of the page says when the data was last updated. If a school's calendar can't be read one night, the last good copy is kept.
- Tests now also check the calendar readers on saved sample pages; the nightly job saves nothing if any test fails.

## 1.0 (November 24, 2026)
- First release: Oak Grove district activities from Nov 23 to Dec 8, 2026 and Central Methodist cheer and STUNT results from the 2025-26 season, in one list.
- Filters by school, type, level, home or away, and date; search; add one or all shown events to a calendar; CSV download; print.
- CMU's 2026-27 cheer schedule was not yet posted when this snapshot was taken.
