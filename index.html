How this works, and tests

Every night a GitHub job reads Oak Grove's EventLink calendar (about 45 days ahead) and Central Methodist's cheer and STUNT calendar feeds, then updates this page's data. If a source can't be read, the last good copy is kept. All times are Central. "Home" and "Away" follow the district's (H) and (A) marks. An event listed twice at two places is shown once with both.
